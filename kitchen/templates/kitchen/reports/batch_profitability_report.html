Batch gross profit is allocated from POS line revenue according to FIFO quantity consumed from that batch, less the batch's FIFO sales COGS. Refunds are excluded from sales; refund-restored stock and prepared-food refund waste are not batch-attributed by the current refund records and therefore may appear in the unreconciled quantity. A non-zero unreconciled quantity on a traceable batch requires review. Batches without a reliably identified finished-goods cost layer are marked Unattributed legacy; their quantities are not presented as confirmed stock discrepancies.
