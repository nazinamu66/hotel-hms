Reporting notes: Store fulfilment counts only StockTransfer records with a matching executed TRANSFER StockMovement. Direct purchase ordered value is calculated from item quantity × unit cost. Received value is calculated from cumulative received quantity × unit cost. Supplier payments are actual Accounting SupplierPayment entries. The received-value-less-payments figure is an operational comparison, not a substitute for the supplier ledger balance. A negative figure indicates payments exceed goods received (a supplier advance or credit), rather than being silently reduced to zero. Staff-funded reimbursements are shown separately and are not included in supplier payments.
